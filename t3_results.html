Results

From the research, It was disovered that Lactobacillus species are often associated with a reduced risk of infections. In the urogenital tract, dominance of Lactobacillus appears linked with a lower incidence of bacterial vaginosis and urinary tract infections. Certain strains, such as Lactobacillus GR-1 and RC-14, have shown promise in restoring and maintaining healthy microbial communities. In the intestinal tract, some Lactobacillus strains have been shown to inhibit the growth of pathogenic bacteria and support gut health. Overall, evidence suggests these bacteria may protect against a range of infections, although the specific mechanisms and effectiveness can vary by strain.
