Methodology

The study involved literature review of published research on the use of Lactobacillus to prevent pathogenic bacterial infections. Then, collecting and analyzing studies that investigated the effects of different Lactobacillus strains on infections in the genital and gut tracts. The study highlighted how these beneficial bacteria interact with pathogens, including by producing hydrogen peroxide and other inhibitory compounds. Then evaluating the findings from both human and animal research to understand how these interactions might translate to practical health benefits.
