Research Objectives

a.) To examine existing research on endophytes as sources of antibiotic compounds.
b.) Identify endophytic microorganisms that can produce metabolites with antibacterial, antifungal, and antiprotozoal activities.
c.) To determine the diversity of endophytes and host plants involved in antibiotic production.
d.) Highlight the potential of endophytes as alternative sources of new antibiotics to address antimicrobial resistance.
