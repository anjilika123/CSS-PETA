Methodology

Reviewing and synthesizing existing research on Penicillium species related to bioremediation was applied. As well as examining studies that reported how different Penicillium strains adsorb, accumulate, or break down pollutants such as heavy metals and toxic organic compounds. With analyzing comparative data on fungal responses to varying environmental conditions that affect pollutant removal efficiency. The collected information was organized to highlight potential mechanisms and practical applications of Penicillium in environmental cleanup.
