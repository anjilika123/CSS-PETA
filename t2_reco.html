Recomendations

Based on the evidences, It is recommended that further experimental studies using Penicillium species in real environmental settings should be done to validate laboratory results. Future researchers also should explore genetic and biochemical pathways that enhance pollutant degradation in these fungi. It would also be useful to test Penicillium in combination with other microbes to improve synergistic bioremediation effects. Finally, It is suggested to develop practical protocols for scaling up fungal bioremediation for contaminated sites.
