Results

Penicillium species have significant capacity to bind and transform various pollutants through cell wall adsorption, production of chelating metabolites, and enzymatic actions. Some strains effectively reduced concentrations of heavy metals like chromium and lead under controlled conditions. The ability to tolerate and process contaminants varied by species and environmental factors such as pH and temperature. These findings suggest Penicillium fungi could play a valuable role in bioremediation strategies, although performance is context-dependent.
