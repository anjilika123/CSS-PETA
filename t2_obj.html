Research Objectives

a.) To investigate the potential of Penicillium species for use in bioremediation of environmental pollutants.
b.) Give focus on how fungi can interact with and transform heavy metals, organic contaminants, and other harmful substances in soil and water.
c.) To identify the functional mechanisms Penicillium species use—such as adsorption and enzymatic breakdown—to reduce pollutant toxicity.
d.) To assess whether Penicillium could be a viable, sustainable option for cleaning contaminated environments.
