Methodology

In this study, literature review method is used to analyze published research on antibiotic-producing endophytes from 2006 to 2016. Then gathering and examining peer-reviewed studies that report the isolation of endophytes from plant tissues. Focus is also given to studies that demonstrate antimicrobial activity against bacterial, fungal, or protozoan pathogens. The collected data are organized based on endophyte type, host plant, and reported bioactivity.
