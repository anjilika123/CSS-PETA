Research Objectives

a.) Explore how Lactobacillus bacteria can help prevent infections by harmful pathogens in the urogenital and intestinal tracts.
b.) Describe the ways that Lactobacillus may protect the body, such as by producing substances that inhibit pathogen growth or by preventing pathogens from sticking to host tissues.
c.) Summarize evidence from different studies on the potential health benefits of specific Lactobacillus strains.
d.) Highlight the promise of these bacteria as alternatives or supplements to conventional medical treatments.
